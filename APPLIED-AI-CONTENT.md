## How each video works

Five beats, in this order, in under 60 seconds.

1. **The result first.** Show the finished thing on screen before anything
   else: the flyer, the email, the spreadsheet chart. People stay for what
   they can see.
2. **The person and the problem.** One named person with a problem the viewer
   has had. "This is Amaka. Amaka spends every Sunday night writing captions."
3. **The free part.** Two or three real steps they can copy today. This has to
   work. If they try it and it works, they trust the rest.
4. **The stop.** Name the part you did not show, and say it is in the class.
   "That gets you a draft. Making it sound like you every time, and running it
   on its own every week, is what we do in the class."
5. **The close.** One keyword. "Comment CLASS and I'll send you the link. It's
   $70 until 20 October."

**Give more than feels comfortable.** Sarah's flyer video worked because the
free part was genuinely useful: 19,000 people commented for the guide. The
free part sells the paid part.

## The ManyChat flow

Your plan is right, and it has already been proven on your own account: the
flyer video asked people to comment "Guide", and 19,400 did. Comment to DM is
the strongest call to action Instagram allows, because a comment also pushes
the video to more people.

How to set it up:

- **One keyword for the class: CLASS.** One word is easy to type and easy to
  count. Use it on every video in this file.
- **The DM gives something before it asks.** Send the free extra you promised
  in the video first (the prompt, the checklist), then the link. A DM that is
  only a sales link feels like bait.
- **The link goes to the class page**, `academy.creovine.com/academy/applied-ai-practitioner`.
  Add `?utm_source=instagram&utm_content=<topic>` so you can see which video
  brought the buyers.
- **Example DM:** "Here's the prompt from the video 👇 [prompt]. The full class
  starts 20 October, and it's $70 until then: [link]"

## Rules for every video

- **Show, don't describe.** Screen recording of the real tool, every time.
- **Real tools only.** Everything below is a tool people can open today.
- **Never promise money.** "Get ahead", "save hours", "win more work". Never
  "make $5,000 a month".
- **Numbers as figures.** "19,000", not "nineteen thousand".
- **Say the price and the date in the close**, not in the middle.
- **One topic per video.** If you have two good ideas, that is two videos.

# Work

## 1. Pick the right AI for the job

**The person.** Tunde uses ChatGPT for everything, and gets average answers
for half of it.

**Hook.** "You're using the wrong AI for half your work."

**The free part.**
1. Writing and long documents: Claude. It holds a long document and keeps your
   tone.
2. Research with sources: Perplexity. Every answer shows where it came from.
3. Anything inside Google Docs or Gmail: Gemini, because it's already there.
4. Quick everyday questions: ChatGPT.

**Where you stop.** "That's the cheat sheet. Knowing which one to trust for
which job, and checking when it's wrong, is week one of the class."

**Tools on screen.** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, side by side with the
same question.

## 2. Emails that sound like you

**The person.** Grace spends her first hour every morning on emails, and the
AI ones sound like a robot wrote them.

**Hook.** "Stop letting AI write emails that sound like AI."

**The free part.**
1. Paste 3 emails you actually sent and liked.
2. Ask: "Describe how I write: tone, length, how I open and close."
3. Then: "Using that style, reply to this email" and paste the new one.

**Where you stop.** "Now do that for every email without pasting anything.
Saving your style once and using it everywhere is in the class."

**Tools on screen.** Claude or ChatGPT, Gmail.

## 3. Research you can trust

**The person.** Kwame asked AI for statistics for a report. Two of them didn't
exist.

**Hook.** "AI made up a statistic and my friend almost sent it to his boss."

**The free part.**
1. Use a tool that shows sources: Perplexity.
2. Click every source before you use the number.
3. Ask the AI: "Which of these claims are you least sure about?"

**Where you stop.** "That catches the obvious ones. A full routine for
checking anything AI tells you, before it goes out with your name on it, is in
the class."

**Tools on screen.** Perplexity, a browser tab with the source open.

## 4. Read a 60 page document in 5 minutes

**The person.** Zainab gets a 60 page report on Friday and has to brief her
manager on Monday.

**Hook.** "I read 60 pages in 5 minutes. Here's how."

**The free part.**
1. Upload the PDF to NotebookLM or Claude.
2. Ask: "Give me the 5 decisions this report wants from me, with the page
   number for each."
3. Ask: "What's the weakest argument in here?"

**Where you stop.** "Comparing three documents at once, and turning them into
a one page brief your boss can act on, is in the class."

**Tools on screen.** NotebookLM, Claude.

## 5. Make sense of a messy spreadsheet

**The person.** David has a sales export with 4,000 rows and no idea what it's
telling him.

**Hook.** "This spreadsheet had the answer. I just couldn't see it."

**The free part.**
1. Upload the file to ChatGPT.
2. Ask: "Clean this up, then tell me the 3 most important things in it."
3. Ask: "Make a chart of the one that matters most."

**Where you stop.** "That's one file, once. Doing the same report every week
without touching it is in the class."

**Tools on screen.** ChatGPT with a spreadsheet, Google Sheets with Gemini.

## 6. Meeting notes that turn into actions

**The person.** Chioma leaves every meeting with pages of notes and no idea who
is doing what.

**Hook.** "My meetings now write their own to-do list."

**The free part.**
1. Record the meeting with a note taker (Otter, Fireflies, or the one built
   into Google Meet or Zoom).
2. Paste the transcript and ask: "List every action, who owns it, and by when."
3. Send that list, not the transcript.

**Where you stop.** "Sending it to everyone and adding it to your task list
automatically is in the class."

**Tools on screen.** Otter or Fireflies, ChatGPT or Claude.

## 7. A presentation in 10 minutes

**The person.** Ibrahim has a pitch tomorrow and an empty slide deck.

**Hook.** "I made this deck in 10 minutes."

**The free part.**
1. Write your point in 5 bullet points.
2. Paste them into Gamma and pick a style.
3. Fix the 2 slides that are wrong, don't fix all of them.

**Where you stop.** "Making it look like your brand and say exactly what you
mean, not what the AI guessed, is in the class."

**Tools on screen.** Gamma, Canva.

## 8. Your own AI assistant that knows your work

**The person.** Funke explains her job to ChatGPT from scratch every single
time.

**Hook.** "Stop explaining yourself to ChatGPT every morning."

**The free part.**
1. Create a Project in Claude or a custom GPT in ChatGPT.
2. Add your job description, your company, and 3 examples of your best work.
3. Every chat in that project now starts already knowing you.

**Where you stop.** "Setting one up for each part of your job, and keeping it
accurate, is in the class."

**Tools on screen.** Claude Projects, ChatGPT custom GPTs.

## 9. Automate the busywork

**The person.** Emeka copies form entries into a spreadsheet and emails each
person back. Every single day.

**Hook.** "This used to take me an hour a day. Now it runs while I sleep."

**The free part.**
1. Pick one task you repeat every day.
2. In Zapier or Make, set the trigger: "When a new form is filled in".
3. Add the step: "Add a row to my sheet", then "Send this email".

**Where you stop.** "Adding AI in the middle, so it reads the message and
decides what to do, is in the class."

**Tools on screen.** Zapier, Make, Google Forms, Google Sheets.

## 10. Build your first AI agent

**The person.** Ada wants a morning briefing on her industry without reading
20 websites.

**Hook.** "I built an AI that does my research before I wake up."

**The free part.**
1. Explain the difference: a chatbot answers, an agent does the steps for you.
2. Show ChatGPT's scheduled tasks or Gemini's scheduled actions: "Every
   morning, find the 3 most important news stories about X and summarise them."
3. Show the result arriving.

**Where you stop.** "That's a simple one. An agent that uses your own tools,
checks its work, and asks you before it acts is what the class builds."

**Tools on screen.** ChatGPT tasks, Gemini, n8n for the advanced view.

## 11. Use AI without leaking your data

**The person.** A bank worker pasted a customer's details into ChatGPT to
write a letter.

**Hook.** "Never paste these 3 things into ChatGPT."

**The free part.**
1. Customer names, account numbers, anything that identifies a person.
2. Your company's private documents, unless your company approved the tool.
3. Passwords and keys. Ever.
4. Bonus: turn off "Improve the model for everyone" in ChatGPT settings.

**Where you stop.** "How to get the same result safely, and what to do when
your company has no rules yet, is in the class."

**Tools on screen.** ChatGPT settings, Data controls.
