## Visa notes

Researched 2 Oct 2026 from GDRFA, ICP, u.ae and Gulf News (28 Sep 2026).

- **Overstay:** AED 50 a day, no grace period. Never let the visa lapse; fines
  also complicate a later change of status.
- **Tourist visa extension:** possible from inside the UAE, but the sponsor
  files it (the travel agency, or the airline if Emirates/flydubai issued it).
  Up to 120 days total. About AED 600–1,150 plus the agency's fee.
  Dubai-issued: gdrfad.gov.ae; other emirates: icp.gov.ae.
- **Job offer → work visa without leaving:** yes. The employer files a change
  of status while the tourist visa is still valid. Takes roughly 2–4 weeks;
  stay on the tourist visa (extended if needed) until it is approved.
- **Job Seeker visa (60/90/120 days):** can be applied for from inside the
  country, no sponsor. Needs MOHRE skill level 1–3 (or a recent top-500
  university degree), a bachelor's or equivalent (the M.D. counts) that is
  **attested**, about AED 900–1,000 in fees plus a refundable AED 1,000
  deposit. The u.ae and GDRFA pages word the eligibility differently; confirm
  at an Amer centre before paying.
- **Freelance visa (fallback that gives residency):** GoFreelance (Dubai
  Internet City) about AED 14–15k first year incl. visa; SHAMS / RAKEZ about
  AED 8–14k. Confirm "software development" is an allowed activity first.
- **Green visa / Golden visa:** not short-term options. Green (self-employed)
  needs ~AED 360k/yr income proof for 2 years; Golden (coders) needs a
  government nomination; Golden (skilled) needs an AED 30k/month UAE job.
  Content-creator Golden visa needs an established audience and a Media
  Council licence.
- **The M.D.:** helps as a degree once attested. Medical visa routes need a
  UAE medical licence.
- **Nigerian passport:** no published rule blocks extension or change of
  status; expect more scrutiny. Restrictions from 2025 were reported eased in
  May 2026.
- **Scams:** employers must pay all visa costs by law (Decree-Law 33/2021).
  Never pay an "employer" for a visa. Check offer letters on the MOHRE app or
  600 590000.

**Attestation is the slow part.** The degree needs attesting in Nigeria, then
by the UAE embassy, then UAE MOFA. Start it now, through someone in Nigeria if
needed; the Job Seeker visa and most work visas need it.
