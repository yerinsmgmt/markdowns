# Motion graphics images

12 images. Paste part 1 once at the top of a new chat. Wait for it to confirm.
Then paste the prompts in part 2, one at a time.

Save every file to the Downloads folder, named by its number: `1.png` for
prompt 1, `2.png` for prompt 2, up to `12.png`. The numbering is the only thing
that has to be right.

---

## Part 1 · Paste this once, at the start of the chat

> Remember these rules for every image I ask you for in this chat. Confirm you
> have them, then wait for my prompts.
>
> Style: paper collage illustration, cut paper edges with visible torn fibre,
> subtle halftone dot texture, flat graphic shapes, no gradients. Editorial,
> like a printed news explainer.
>
> Palette: off white, warm sand, deep charcoal near black, and a single
> desaturated teal accent. Nothing else. High contrast, bold, with generous
> empty space.
>
> Never include: text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, glowing, neon,
> circuit boards, robots, holograms, photorealism, 3D renders, or drop shadows.
>
> I will start each prompt with either BOARD or ELEMENT.
>
> When I write BOARD: make it wide 16:9, 1920x1080. Put the subject off centre
> and leave at least a third of the frame quiet and empty.
>
> When I write ELEMENT: put the subject alone and centred on a completely flat
> pure magenta background, hex FF00FF, with no gradient, no texture and no
> shadow falling on the background. Nothing in the subject may be magenta or
> pink. Leave a clear margin of at least ten percent on every side and never
> let the subject touch an edge. Square, 1024x1024.
